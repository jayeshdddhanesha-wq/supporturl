Frequently Asked Questions

Can I choose different chat bubble styles?

Yes! You can pick styles in the chat settings. Adjust the bubble shape or corner roundness to make your story look unique.

How are my chats saved?

Your chats are stored right on your phone using local storage. The app saves your messages, names, and images automatically so you can pick up where you left off.

How do I manage my chat list?

In the main screen, you can search for chats, archive old ones, pin favorites to the top, or delete them. Just swipe or long-press on a chat to see options.

Why does exporting a video take time?

Exporting videos can take longer if your story has many messages or images. Each message needs to be turned into a video frame, so a longer chat means a bit more waiting. Try keeping stories under 50 messages for faster exports!

How do I create a new chat session?

Tap the 'New Chat' button, pick a name and profile picture from your photos, and start adding messages. It's easy to make a fresh story!

How do I add messages to a chat?

In the chat screen, type your message, choose if it's from you or the character, and tap send. You can also add images or typing bubbles for realism.

How do I start a chat story?

Open the app, tap a character (like Anna for horror), and tap to reveal their messages one word at a time. It’s like reading a story through a chat!

How do I create a fake chat?

Pick a chat style (like WhatsApp or iMessage), add your story’s messages, and customize them with colors or backgrounds. Then export as a picture or video!

Can I change how the chat looks?

Yes! Choose a chat style (like WhatsApp or iMessage), pick bubble colors, or add a cool background. Tap the settings icon in the chat screen to make it your own.

How do I save or share my story?

Finish your story, then tap the export button to save it as a picture or video to your photos. Share it on Instagram, TikTok, WhatsApp, or anywhere!

Can I share my stories on social media?

Absolutely! After exporting your chat story as a picture or video, share it directly to Instagram, TikTok, WhatsApp, or other platforms.

Why don’t I hear sounds for messages or calls?

Check if the mute button is on in the app. Also, make sure your phone’s volume is up and not on silent. Contact us if you still have trouble.

Why isn’t my exported video working?

Make sure your video is 15-30 seconds and exported at 30 fps. Check your phone’s storage or try again. Email us if it’s still not working.

Is this app for real chatting?

No, it’s all for fun! The app creates fake chat stories with made-up characters for storytelling, not real messaging.

How do I start a simulated call?

Pick a character in the app, then tap the call button in the chat screen. You'll see a ringing screen with options to mute, turn on speaker, or hold. It's all for fun storytelling!

What does clearing the cache do?

It removes temporary files like old images and videos to free up space on your phone. Go to settings and tap 'Clear Cache' if the app feels slow.
