Frequently Asked Questions

How do I start a new story?

Open the app → tap any character profile → the story starts automatically. Tap the screen to reveal messages word-by-word, just like real texting!

How do I choose my reply?

When options appear at the bottom, tap the reply you want. Your choice changes the story — sometimes dramatically!

What are Chat Master Challenges?

Special levels where you have to pick the perfect reply to win. Complete 50+ challenges to prove you’re a true Text Master!

Why can’t I skip the ads?

Ads keep the app 100% free and let us add new stories every week. Thank you for your understanding!

Can I replay a story with different choices?

Yes! Go back to the home screen, tap the same character again, and the story restarts so you can try different replies and see new endings.

Are these real people texting me?

Nope — everything is 100% fictional and created for fun. But it feels super real, right?

Will there be more stories?

Yes! We add new exciting stories every week — horror, romance, thriller, comedy, and more.

The app is lagging or crashing

Try force-closing the app and reopening. If it keeps happening, email us with your iPhone model and iOS version — we’ll fix it fast.

We love that! Reply to this email with your idea — the best ones might become real stories (with credit to you).
